Q: what is ftp

Q: what is an exception

Q: what is a try catch block

Q: what is a syntax error

Q: what is a runtime error
A: a runtime error occurs while a program is running, often due to invalid operations.

Q: what is staging
A: staging is a pre-production environment used for final testing before deployment.

Q: what is load balancing

Q: what is scalability
